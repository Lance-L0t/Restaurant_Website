A Journey Through Fire

Founded on passion, fueled by flavor.

The Philosophy

At Meat & Grill, we believe that fire is more than just a tool—it's an ingredient. Our journey started in a small backyard with a single goal: to perfect the art of the fire-roasted chicken. We spent years sourcing the finest orange-red spices and mastering the precise timing required to achieve a flawless char.

Today, that same obsession drives everything we do. From the volcanic stone in our ovens to the locally-sourced citrus in our marinades, every element is chosen to elevate the dining experience.
